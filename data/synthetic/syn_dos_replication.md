# Replicating DoS Classifiers on Real Traffic (SYNTHETIC FIXTURE - not a real paper)
## Motivation
Reported DoS detection accuracies are often measured on synthetic datasets.
## Results
When we retrained a gradient boosted classifier on captured operator traffic, accuracy dropped to 84 percent. False positives were concentrated in legitimate flash crowds, such as stadium events.
## Discussion
The gap suggests that synthetic benchmarks overstate real-world detection performance.
