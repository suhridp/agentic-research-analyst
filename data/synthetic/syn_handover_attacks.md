# Spoofing Attacks on Data-Driven Handover (SYNTHETIC FIXTURE - not a real paper)
## Threat model
An attacker controls a rogue device that sends spoofed measurement reports to the serving base station. The goal is to steer a data-driven handover predictor into selecting a rogue cell.
## Results
Adversarial perturbation of RSRP values by 6 dB flipped 40 percent of handover predictions in the simulated study. Perturbations below 2 dB had no measurable effect on predictions.
## Discussion
Authentication of measurement reports reduces the attack surface but adds signaling latency. Rogue base stations remain a threat during inter-RAT transitions.
