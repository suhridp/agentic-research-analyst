## Methods
We compare adversarial training, anomaly detection on measurement reports, and ensemble predictors.
## Results
Anomaly detection on measurement reports flagged 92 percent of spoofed reports at a 3 percent false positive rate. Adversarial training improved prediction robustness under perturbed measurements but reduced clean accuracy by 1.5 points. Ensemble predictors reduce sensitivity to a single manipulated input.
## Discussion
Defenses that add signaling overhead must be weighed against handover latency budgets.
