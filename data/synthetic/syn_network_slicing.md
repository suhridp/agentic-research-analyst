# Isolation in Network Slicing (SYNTHETIC FIXTURE - not a real paper)
## Background
Network slicing partitions a 5G network into logical slices with separate quality of service guarantees.
## Results
Under a noisy-neighbour load test, latency in the protected slice increased by 12 ms when isolation was disabled and by less than 1 ms when it was enabled.
## Discussion
Slice isolation does not by itself protect against attacks on shared control plane functions.
