# Predictive Handover with Recurrent Models (SYNTHETIC FIXTURE - not a real paper)
## Abstract
Predictive handover uses radio measurements such as RSRP and RSRQ to anticipate cell changes before signal quality degrades. We train a recurrent model on mobility traces collected along urban and highway routes.
## Results
The recurrent model reduced handover failures by 18 percent in the simulated urban scenario. On highway traces the reduction was smaller, at 7 percent, because cell dwell times were shorter. Prediction errors cause ping-pong handovers and radio link failure.
## Limitations
The evaluation used simulated traces only and did not include inter-RAT handover between 5G and 4G.
