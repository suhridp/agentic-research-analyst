# Early DoS Detection in 5G Cores (SYNTHETIC FIXTURE - not a real paper)
## Approach
Early detection of denial-of-service traffic in 5G cores relies on flow statistics gathered at the user plane function.
## Results
A gradient boosted classifier detected DoS floods with 97 percent accuracy on a synthetic dataset. Detection latency was under 200 ms for volumetric floods.
## Discussion
Signaling storms can exhaust control plane resources. Rate limiting mitigates floods but may block legitimate bursts.
